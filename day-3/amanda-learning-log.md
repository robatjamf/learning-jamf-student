**If you were telling a friend about this workshop, what would you say about working with AI?**

>I would tell them how working with AI is all about you the prompt. If your prompts are well structured and well thought out, your results are more likely to be what you want instead of just letting the agent blindly try and make decisions without you helping it. It is not their fault when you get something you did not want due to being vague.

**Which principle or idea from today will you remember?**

>Working with AI is a conversation not a command line. Collaborating, refining and asking questions is what gets you to what you want.
