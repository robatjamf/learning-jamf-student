DAY 1 — WELCOME & DISCOVERY





Icebreakers & Cultural Exchange


Told the whole room I fly my car over traffic blasting Afrobeats. Silence for 10 seconds. Then questions. Best icebreaker of my life. Shared the story of morning walks with Chomps at Victoria Falls — honey badger walking is apparently not a common hobby. Met people from everywhere. Felt like a music festival with more laptops.







What is Jamf?


Jamf is the ultimate remote control for Apple devices. 10,000 Macs running wild? Jamf whistles and they all line up. Like training Chomps — except Jamf actually listens. It's the backstage crew making sure the whole concert runs smooth while everyone else enjoys the show.







Persona Mapping — K-12 IT Coordinator


We mapped Ms. Rivera — one IT person, 400 students, zero budget, infinite chaos. Biggest pain: devices going missing and students installing Minecraft on school iPads. My solution: a one-tap "exam mode" button with a cinematic bass drop. I would score the audio myself.

DAY 2 — APIs, VMs & WEBHOOKS








Claude Code


Expected fancy autocomplete. Got a co-pilot who knows every instrument and never complains. Ran /init — it read the whole project and summarised it better than I could. Like jamming with a musician who can play everything. Chomps could never.






VMs — Computer Inside a Computer


Imagine your flying car has a mini flying car in the glove box. Same engine, own GPS, own playlist. You crash the mini car — your real car keeps flying. That's a Tart VM. Commands: tart run, tart list, SSH.










APIs — Music Streaming Analogy


API = music streaming service. I request a track (data). Platform fetches from the catalogue. I don't need to know where the studio is. POST requests are like submitting a demo — sending something new and hoping it gets accepted.






Webhooks — Push vs Pull


Pull = refreshing my feed hoping a new album dropped. Push = getting a text the SECOND it drops. Webhooks are push. Payload arrived as clean JSON: event, device, timestamp. Chomps delivers messages faster but with more bite marks.

DAY 3 — BUILDING WITH AI





The One-Shot Build


A unicorn born already knowing how to trot. Looked decent, had the right bones, but was missing things I never said out loud. The AI built what I described — not what I imagined. The gap between "good enough" and "what I actually meant" was the whole lesson.







The Planned Build


Spent the most time writing the brief. Felt unnatural — like planning a route through Victoria Falls mist before Chomps starts pulling the leash. But the planned build was smoother. Every section had a reason. Planning forces decisions before they bite you.







The Big Takeaway


"The more clearly you think, the more powerful the tool becomes. AI is a fast collaborator that needs you to bring the vision. You're still the artist. AI is just the producer who can lay down any track instantly."
