## After Block 2 — What Is Jamf?

**In your own words, what does Jamf do?**

> Jamf is basically the ultimate remote control for Apple devices. Imagine you have a herd of 10,000 MacBooks running wild across a company — Jamf is what lets IT wrangle them all without physically touching each one. It's like being able to whistle and have every device line up perfectly, kind of like how I trained Chomps... except Jamf actually listens.

**What surprised you about Jamf's products or integrations?**

> I had no idea Jamf connected to so many other tools — Microsoft, Google, security platforms, identity providers. I assumed it was just "push apps to Macs" but it's basically the backstage crew making sure the whole concert runs smoothly. As someone who produces music, I respect that level of behind-the-scenes coordination.
