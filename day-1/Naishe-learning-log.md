## After Block 2 — What Is Jamf?

**In your own words, what does Jamf do?**

>Jamf is an Apple-focused device management and security platform used by companies, schools, and governments to deploy, configure, monitor, and protect Apple devices at scale. It’s in the same general category as MDM/UEM tools, but specializes in Apple ecosystems.

**What surprised you about Jamf's products or integrations?**

>I expected a single admin console for pushing profiles and apps, but Jamf has a full suite: Jamf Pro, Jamf Now, Jamf School, Jamf Protect, Jamf Connect, Jamf Safe Internet, Executive Threat Protection, and more. That’s a lot of surface area for what I initially thought was “the Mac MDM company.” It’s closer to being an Apple-only equivalent of an endpoint/security platform than a simple management tool.
